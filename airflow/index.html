Training aid only

Airflow Trainer is a simplified simulation for learning how a forced-air system moves air: static pressure, blower and system curves, room balancing, and what the coil does to temperature and moisture. It is not a substitute for training, ACCA Manuals J, S and D, or the equipment's own blower tables.

  • Numbers are typical for a 3-ton system on a 60,000 Btu/h furnace with ducts in the attic; the physics is simplified. Always use the manufacturer's blower table and installation manual.
  • Turn the power off before pulling a limit switch or drilling test ports, and never drill into a coil, heat exchanger or near heat strips.
  • The failure scenarios are sped up to show how airflow problems stack.
