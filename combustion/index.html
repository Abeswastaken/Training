Training aid only

Combustion and Gas Trainer is a simplified simulation for learning how gas piping, regulators, orifices, combustion air and venting work together, and how to read a combustion analyzer. It is not a substitute for gas-fitter licensing, the fuel gas code (IFGC/NFPA 54), or the appliance manufacturer's instructions.

  • Pipe sizing uses the IFGC low-pressure formula for schedule 40 steel; always size from the code tables your jurisdiction adopts.
  • Real gas work needs a licensed fitter, a leak check with an approved solution or detector, and a calibrated analyzer. Never use a flame to find a leak.
  • Carbon monoxide kills. If a CO alarm sounds, get everyone out, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside.
  • The failure scenarios are sped up to show how combustion and venting problems stack.
