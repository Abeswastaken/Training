How to

Log

Training aid only

Wiring Trainer is a simplified simulation for practicing control and power wiring. It is not a design tool or a substitute for training, the equipment's wiring diagram, or the electrical code.

  • The circuit math is simplified (single-phase AC treated as RMS values; motors as fixed loads).
  • Real arc flash and shock can kill. De-energize, lock out, tag out, and verify dead with a tested meter before you touch wiring.
