Training aid only

These pages explain how equipment works in plain language, with simplified drawings and a simple model. They are not a substitute for the manufacturer's manual, your plant's procedures, or your instructor.

  • Numbers are typical values for learning. Your machine's real numbers are on its startup sheet and in its manual.
  • Real equipment runs on high voltage, holds refrigerant, water or steam under pressure, and may burn gas. Follow lockout/tagout and your site's safety rules.
