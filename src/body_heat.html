Training aid only

Heating Trainer is a simplified simulation for learning how a gas furnace's parts and safeties work alone and together. It is not a substitute for training, the manufacturer's instructions, a combustion analyzer, or your local code.

  • Numbers are typical for an 80,000 BTU/h natural gas furnace and the physics is simplified. Always set up and diagnose from the furnace's own data plate and installation manual.
  • Gas work requires the license your area requires. Never bypass a limit, rollout or pressure switch on real equipment. A confirmed cracked heat exchanger means shut it down and red-tag it.
  • The failure scenarios are sped up to show why each safety matters.
