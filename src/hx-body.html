Training aid only

Heat Exchangers is a simplified simulator for learning how exchangers move heat and why they're piped the way they are. It is not a sizing or selection tool.

  • Temperatures come from a textbook model with typical numbers. Real units follow the manufacturer's data and selection software.
  • Hot water, steam and pressurized vessels can scald and burst. Isolate, drain, cool and relieve pressure before opening any exchanger.
