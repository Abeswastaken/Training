Training aid only

Chiller and Cooling Tower Trainer is a simplified simulation of a 300-ton centrifugal chiller plant: chilled water loop, refrigerant circuit, condenser water and an open cooling tower. It teaches how approaches, lift, surge and tower water treatment fit together. It is not a substitute for the chiller manufacturer's operation and maintenance manual or for refrigerant certification.

  • Numbers follow AHRI 550/590 rating conditions (44/54°F chilled water, 85/95°F condenser water) and typical R-134a pressures; your machine's log sheet and design values are what count.
  • Lock out the chiller, pumps and tower fan before opening water boxes, strainers or the fan deck. Tower water can carry Legionella: wear a respirator when working in the spray.
  • The failure scenarios are sped up to show how plant problems stack.
